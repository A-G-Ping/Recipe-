# Master Recipe Cookbook

**Exhaustive Family Heirlooms & Wanda Ping Edition — All 131 Recipes**

A beautiful, searchable, interactive digital cookbook preserving 131 cherished family recipes.

## Features
- 🔍 Live search by recipe name, ingredient, or keyword
- 📂 Category filters (Cakes & Baking, Colorado & Tex-Mex, Holiday Turkey, Desserts, and more)
- 📱 Fully responsive design
- 📖 Full recipe details in elegant modal view (ingredients, directions, kitchen notes)
- 🎨 Warm parchment aesthetic with classic typography
- 🌙 **Dark mode toggle** (sun/moon button in the top-right corner)

## Dark Mode
A dark mode toggle is available in the top-right of the header.

- Click the **sun ☀️ / moon 🌙** button to switch themes
- Your preference is automatically saved in the browser (localStorage)
- On first visit it also respects your system dark/light preference

## Live Site
Once GitHub Pages is enabled, the cookbook will be available at:

**https://a-g-ping.github.io/Recipe-/**

## How to Enable GitHub Pages (to make it a live website)
1. Go to the repository: https://github.com/A-G-Ping/Recipe-
2. Click **Settings** → **Pages** (left sidebar)
3. Under **Source**, select **Deploy from a branch**
4. Choose branch: `main` and folder: `/ (root)`
5. Click **Save**

After a minute or two, your cookbook will be live!

## Make the Repository Public (optional)
1. Go to **Settings** → **General**
2. Scroll to **Danger Zone**
3. Click **Change visibility** → **Make public**
